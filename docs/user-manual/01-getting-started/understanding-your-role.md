# Understanding Your Role

> **Role:** Everyone

## Overview

The platform shows you only the pages and actions that match your role. Your role label is shown in the left sidebar under your email address.

## The roles at a glance

- **Appraisee** — view and act on your own appraisal (self-assessment, comments, sign-off).
- **Appraisor** — everything an Appraisee does, plus rate, comment on, and sign off your direct reports' appraisals.
- **HR Admin** — full access: cycles, users, reports, audit log, and every appraisal in the organisation.

The platform also recognises **HR Director** (read-only HR access for delegated staff) and **Executive** (read-only access to dashboards and reports).

For the full capability matrix and detailed per-role descriptions, see [Role Permissions](../05-reference/role-permissions.md).

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I have more than one role?**
A: Yes. For example, a department head is usually both **Appraisee** and **Appraisor**. The sidebar combines the menus for all your roles.

**Q: How do I change my role?**
A: Only HR Admins can change roles. Contact your HR administrator.
