# Role Permissions

> **Role:** Everyone
> **Audience:** Appraisees, Appraisors, HR Admins

## Overview

The platform recognises five roles. Each user can have one or more roles; the menus and actions shown are the union of all assigned roles.

## Role descriptions

### Appraisee

The default role. Every staff member with an account is an Appraisee. Appraisees can:

- View their own appraisals (current and historical).
- Submit their self-assessment.
- Read manager ratings during Discussion.
- Add comments.
- View their growth plan.
- Sign off (accept or reject).
- Manage their own password and MFA.

### Appraisor

Granted to anyone with at least one direct report. In addition to Appraisee capabilities, Appraisors can:

- See an **Employees** menu listing direct reports.
- Open each direct report's appraisal.
- Rate Key Deliverables and Competencies.
- Add manager comments.
- Build the growth plan.
- Sign off the manager signature.

### HR Director

A read-only HR support role. Useful for HR coordinators who help with cycle administration but should not have full admin rights. HR Directors can:

- View any employee's appraisal.
- View the audit log.
- View analytics dashboards.
- Cannot create cycles, edit users, or finalise.

### HR Admin

The full administrative role. Owns appraisal cycles, employee records, user accounts, and reporting. HR Admins can do everything HR Directors do, plus:

- Create, activate, close, and finalise cycles.
- Add, edit, and deactivate users.
- Reopen Incomplete appraisals.
- Exclude employees from cycles.
- Bulk import historical appraisals.

### Executive

Read-only access to dashboards and analytics — nothing more. Designed for leadership who need visibility into organisation-wide performance trends without operational responsibilities.

## Role assignment

HR Admins assign roles when creating or editing a user. See [Managing users and roles](../04-hr-admin-guide/managing-users.md) for details.

## Common Questions

**Q: I report to two managers. Which one rates me?**
A: The platform supports one direct manager per employee. If you have a dotted-line manager, your formal manager is whoever appears as "Manager" on your employee profile — only they can rate you.

**Q: Can I be both Appraisor and HR Admin?**
A: Yes. The role checkboxes at user creation allow any combination. The sidebar menus combine accordingly.

**Q: How do I get my role changed?**
A: Ask your HR Admin. Self-service role changes are not allowed.
