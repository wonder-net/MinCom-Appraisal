# MINCOM Performance Appraisal — User Manual

Welcome to the MINCOM Employee Performance Appraisal Platform. This manual walks you through every feature available to you based on your role.

## Who is this for?

The platform supports three primary roles:

- **Appraisee** — Anyone whose performance is being appraised in a cycle.
- **Appraisor** — Anyone with at least one direct report. Appraisors are also appraisees in their own appraisals.
- **HR Admin** — Owns appraisal cycles, employee records, user accounts, and reporting.

If you are unsure which role applies to you, check the small label under your name in the left sidebar after you sign in.

## How to use this manual

- Each section is organised by role. Start with **Getting Started**, then jump to the section for your role.
- Every workflow is shown with screenshots taken from the live platform.
- The **Reference** section explains the scoring methodology, workflow stages, and what each role can do.
