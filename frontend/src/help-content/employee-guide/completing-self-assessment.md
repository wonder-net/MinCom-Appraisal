# Completing Your Self-Assessment

> **Role:** Employee

## Overview

When a cycle has self-rating enabled, you complete a self-assessment before your manager reviews you. You score each Key Deliverable and each Behavioural Competency on a 1-to-5 scale and add comments.

> 💡 **Tip:** You can save and return any time before submitting. Nothing is shared with your manager until you click **Submit Self-Assessment**.

## Steps

### Step 1: Open your self-assessment

From the **Appraisals** page, find the row with status **Self Assessment** and click **View**.

![Appraisal in Self Assessment status with workflow progress bar](../../screenshots/employee/02-appraisal-detail-key-deliverables.png)

### Step 2: Add or rate your Key Deliverables

The **Key Deliverables** tab is grouped into four Balanced Scorecard perspectives — Financial, Customer, Internal Business Processes, and Learning and Growth.

For each perspective:

1. If your form is empty, click **+ Add key deliverable** to add one.
2. Type the description and the weight (a percentage of the perspective).
3. Pick your rating from 1 (Below Standard) to 5 (Outstanding) under **Self Rating**.
4. The **Weighted Score** column updates automatically.

The total weight across all four perspectives must add up to 100% before you can submit.

![Key Deliverables grouped by perspective with weights and ratings](../../screenshots/employee/02-appraisal-detail-key-deliverables.png)

### Step 3: Rate your Competencies

Open the **Competencies** tab. You see a list of behavioural competencies that match your form (Form A for managers, Form B for non-managers).

Pick a self-rating from 1 to 5 for each competency. Skip any competency that does not apply.

![Competencies tab showing self and manager ratings side by side](../../screenshots/employee/03-appraisal-competencies.png)

### Step 4: Add your comments

Open the **Comments** tab. Use this space to provide context for your ratings — examples of work delivered, challenges, or anything you want your manager to know during the review.

![Comments tab showing employee and manager comment threads](../../screenshots/employee/04-appraisal-comments.png)

### Step 5: Submit your self-assessment

When you are happy with your ratings and comments, click **Submit Self-Assessment** at the top of the page.

> ⚠️ **Warning:** Once you submit, you cannot edit your self-ratings. Review carefully first.

## What Happens Next

- The appraisal moves to **Manager Review** status.
- Your manager receives an email and an in-app notification.
- Your manager rates you against the same Key Deliverables and Competencies. You will see their ratings once the appraisal moves to **Discussion**.

## Common Questions

**Q: I have nothing to put in a perspective. Can I leave it empty?**
A: No — all four perspectives must have at least the weights filled in, and the total must reach 100%. Speak to your manager if you are unsure how to weight your role.

**Q: What does the cycle "No Self Rating" mean?**
A: It means HR has switched off self-rating for that cycle. You skip the self-assessment stage and your manager rates you directly.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Cannot click Submit | Weights do not total 100% | Check each perspective and adjust |
| Submit fails with validation error | Some fields are empty | The form highlights missing rows in red |
| Cannot edit a rating | The appraisal has already moved to Manager Review | Talk to your manager — changes can be made through the Discussion stage |
