# Reading Notifications

> **Role:** Everyone

## Overview

The bell icon in the top bar tells you when something has happened that needs your attention — for example a new appraisal stage, a comment from your manager, or a sign-off request. Notifications also arrive by email, but the bell keeps an in-app record.

## Steps

### Step 1: Spot the unread badge

If you have unread notifications, a number appears next to the bell. The notification button label reads "Notifications — N unread".

### Step 2: Open the notifications panel

Click the bell. A panel slides out with your most recent notifications.

![Notifications panel listing recent appraisal events](../../screenshots/employee/09-notifications.png)

### Step 3: Read and follow up

Each entry shows:

- **Title** — what happened (for example "Sign-Off Requested" or "Discussion Completed").
- **Description** — a short explanation, often listing the status change.
- **Time** — relative timestamp ("1 day ago").
- **View appraisal** link — jumps straight to the affected appraisal.

Click **View appraisal** to go to the relevant page. The notification is then marked as read.

## What Happens Next

- Reading a notification clears the unread badge.
- The platform keeps a history of recent notifications so you can scroll back through past events.

## Common Questions

**Q: Why am I getting emails as well as in-app notifications?**
A: The platform sends both for important events (sign-off requests, status changes) so you don't miss them when away from the platform.

**Q: Can I turn off notifications?**
A: In-app notifications are always on. Contact HR if your email volume is too high — they can adjust which events trigger emails for your role.
