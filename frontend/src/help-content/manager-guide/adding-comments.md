# Adding Manager Comments

> **Role:** Manager

## Overview

Comments give context to your ratings. They are visible to the employee, HR Admins, and anyone with read access to the appraisal. Both you and the employee must add at least one comment before the appraisal can move from **Discussion** to **Growth Planning**.

## Steps

### Step 1: Open the Comments tab

From the appraisal, click the **Comments** tab.

![Comments tab showing manager and employee comment threads](../../screenshots/manager/07-comments.png)

### Step 2: Read what is already there

The Comments tab shows two threads side by side or stacked, depending on screen width:

- **Appraisee comments** — written by the appraisee.
- **Appraisor comments** — written by you.

### Step 3: Add your comment

Type your comment into the **Appraisor comment** input. Keep it factual and constructive — call out specific examples when possible.

> 💡 **Tip:** Comments support multiple lines. Use them to summarise the conversation, capture agreed actions, and acknowledge anything the employee raised.

Click **Add comment** (or **Save**) to record it.

### Step 4: Add additional comments as the discussion unfolds

You can add as many comments as you need throughout the **Discussion** stage. Each comment is timestamped and attributed to the author.

## What Happens Next

- Once both you and the employee have added at least one comment, the appraisal can be moved to **Growth Planning** by clicking the appropriate button.
- All comments stay visible for the rest of the appraisal lifecycle and are included in the PDF download.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I edit a comment after I've saved it?**
A: No. Comments are immutable once saved. Add a follow-up comment instead.

**Q: Are comments private?**
A: No. Comments are visible to the employee, your manager, HR, and anyone reviewing the appraisal. Treat them as a formal record.
